Accessibility Shouldn't Be an Afterthought

For millions of people who are blind, low-vision, deaf, or hard of hearing, going to the movies has never been as simple as buying a ticket. Audio description narrates the visual moments a film doesn't explain in dialogue. Closed captions make every line, sound, and cue readable. Our app brings both to your pocket — synced precisely with the film you're watching, wherever you're watching it.

We don't stream movies. We deliver the missing piece: the audio and text tracks that make the movie experience whole.

Every Story Deserves to Be Fully Experienced

Despite legal mandates in many countries requiring accessible media, actually finding and using audio description and captions at the moment you need them — in a theater seat, mid-film — remains difficult.

We built this app to close that gap: giving people a simple way to access the accessibility tracks that already exist, right when and where they need them.

We Hold This Site to the Same Standard

Before we ask you to trust ADCC PRO with your movie night, this site should work for you too.

  • Full Keyboard Navigation

    Every link, button, and control — including the FAQ accordion and the sync slider — is reachable and operable by keyboard alone, with a visible gold focus outline.

  • Screen Reader Support

    Semantic HTML, descriptive alt text, and ARIA labels so screen readers announce every control accurately, not just the visuals.

  • WCAG AA Color Contrast

    Text and interactive elements are checked against our dark background to stay readable for low-vision users.

  • Reduced Motion Respected

    If your system requests reduced motion, our scroll animations and transitions turn off automatically.

  • Skip Navigation Link

    A "Skip to main content" link lets keyboard and screen reader users jump straight past the header.
